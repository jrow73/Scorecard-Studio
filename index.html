Welcome to Scorecard Studio

Your scorecard. Game ready.

Upload your favorite baseball scorecard and print it with game-day information already filled in. Scorecard Studio is a local-browser workspace. No account is required.

Layouts stay in this browserYour layouts and uploaded PDF templates are not uploaded to a Scorecard Studio data service.
Baseball data comes from MLBThe app requests schedules, lineups, statistics, and other game information from the MLB Stats API.
Backups are your portable copyDownload a backup when you want to protect or move your layouts and settings.

The normal site host still serves the application files needed to open Scorecard Studio.
